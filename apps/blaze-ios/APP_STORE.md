## Description (4000)

Blaze Wallet is a self-custody wallet for Stacks, made by Charisma. Your keys stay on your iPhone, in the Keychain behind Face ID, and never leave it.

SEE EVERYTHING YOU HOLD
• Your total in dollars, with its 24-hour change
• Every Stacks token you hold, with live balances
• Your NFTs and your activity, each a tap from the explorer

SWAP AT THE BEST ROUTE
• Charisma's router finds the best route across Stacks pools
• Every pool on a route is checked against Charisma's vault list before anything is built
• The review shows what you send, what you get and the least you'll receive, before you sign

BLAZE
• Any token with a Blaze subnet gets a Standard / Blaze switch on each side of a trade
• Upgrade a token into Blaze, or downgrade it back, in one step
• Trades from Blaze are signed orders a solver fills in about a minute, with no network fee for you

SEND WITH CERTAINTY
• Token sends carry a post-condition: exactly the amount you confirmed leaves your wallet, nothing else
• The real network fee is shown before you send

YOURS, ON THIS IPHONE
• Create a wallet, or import a 12- or 24-word seed phrase
• Several seed phrases and accounts in one wallet
• Locks itself after 15 idle minutes
• No account, no analytics, no tracking

Charisma's Dark · RPG and Light · Bitcoin looks, following your iPhone or the one you pick. In English, Korean, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Turkish, Japanese and Portuguese.

Blaze Wallet is a tool for managing your own crypto. Prices move, and blockchain transactions can't be undone.

>
> To look around: open the app and choose Create a new wallet (write down the 24 words it shows, then confirm three of them).
>

> The app talks only to Hiro's public Stacks API (api.hiro.so) and Charisma's services (*.charisma.rocks).

## App Privacy (draft: confirm before submitting)

Tracking: **No**. No analytics or advertising SDKs.

Data collected, both **linked to the user** and used only for **App Functionality**:

| Type | What and why |
| --- | --- |
| Identifiers › User ID | The wallet's public Stacks address: the balance service keeps it in caches for under an hour, and a Blaze order stores it with the order |
| Financial Info › Other Financial Info | A Blaze order's tokens, amount and signature, kept by Charisma's order service so a solver can fill it |

Not collected: contact info, location, contacts, photos, browsing history, diagnostics, usage data.
